RD-IMM: Reference-Filter-Driven Transition Probabilities for IMM-Based Satellite Maneuver Detection

Euiseok Han, Sangheon Choi, Seung-Hyun KongPreprint · arXiv:2610.11242 ↗

The interacting multiple model (IMM) filter tracks a satellite with a coast model and a maneuver model and mixes them through a transition probability matrix (TPM). Adaptive-TPM methods update the TPM from statistics computed inside the IMM, which already depend on the TPM through the mixing step. RD-IMM drives the coast-to-maneuver probability pCM from the normalized innovation squared (NIS) of a reference coast filter that is not mixed with the IMM.

Monte Carlo runs

A geostationary target is tracked by four space-based optical sensors (azimuth and elevation every 60 s). The truth includes J2–J6, lunisolar third-body and solar radiation pressure accelerations, whereas all filters propagate J2 only. A single impulse is applied at tb = 120 min, and the first 30 min are excluded as warm-up.
